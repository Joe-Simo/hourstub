Hourstub

Privacy Policy

Hourstub does not collect any data. Your jobs and shifts stay on your iPhone.

Effective October 26, 2026.

What Hourstub stores

Your jobs, pay rates, overtime rules, shifts, breaks, tips, and notes are stored only on your device, in the app's own storage and in an App Group shared with Hourstub's widgets and Live Activity. This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us or to anyone else.

What Hourstub does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising.
  • No third-party SDKs.
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera access.

CSV export

If you use CSV export (Pro), Hourstub creates a file on your device and hands it to the share sheet. Where it goes after that is your choice.

Purchases

Hourstub Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, email, or payment details. The app checks your purchase with Apple on your device so it can unlock Pro features. Apple's handling of purchase information is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Siri, Shortcuts, widgets, and Live Activities

These features run on your device using Apple's system frameworks. Hourstub does not send their data anywhere.

Backups and deleting your data

If you use iCloud Backup or a computer backup, your device backup may include Hourstub's data, under Apple's terms. To delete everything Hourstub stores, delete the app from your iPhone.

Children

Hourstub does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
