Tracking a shift

Tap Clock In when your shift starts. Tap Break and End Break for unpaid breaks, then Clock Out. To add a shift you forgot, tap + in the toolbar. Tap any shift to edit its times, break, tips, or note.

Overtime and pay periods

Open Manage Jobs and choose a job to set weekly and daily overtime thresholds, the overtime multiplier, your workweek start day, and your pay period. Daily overtime is counted first, and those hours do not count toward weekly overtime again. Amounts are gross estimates before taxes and deductions. Always check your official pay stub.

Live Activity and widgets

With Hourstub Pro, a Live Activity appears on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island when you clock in, with Break and Clock Out buttons. To add a Home Screen or Lock Screen widget, touch and hold your Home Screen or Lock Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and choose Hourstub.

Siri and the Action Button

With Hourstub Pro, say "Clock in with Hourstub", "Clock out with Hourstub", or "How much have I earned in Hourstub". You can also assign an Hourstub shortcut to the Action Button in Settings, Action Button, Shortcut.

Purchases

Hourstub Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. If Pro isn't unlocked after buying, open Settings in Hourstub and tap Restore Purchase while signed in with the same Apple Account. For refund requests, visit Apple's purchase support.

Your data

Hourstub keeps your jobs and shifts only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes its data. See the Privacy Policy.
